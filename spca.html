ABOUT SPCA CANTERBURY

The SPCA of Canterbury is dedicated to the rescue, rehabilitation, and rehoming of cats and kittens throughout the region. SPCA's mission is to provide shelter, veterinary care, and love to the vulnerable animals that come to them. Their staff work tirelessly to find them permanent homes. Here you can learn about their services and how they are making a positive impact.
